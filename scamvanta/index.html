ScamVanta is an independent educational project. All scenarios, names, messages, and addresses are fictional and are provided for educational purposes only. Never interact with a suspicious message or link. Verify concerns through an organization’s official website or phone number.

Educational practice cannot guarantee protection from every scam.

Optional usage statistics

With your permission, Google Analytics receives page views, quiz starts and completions, category and lesson selections, answer category/difficulty/correctness, retry actions, and numeric quiz scores and accuracy. No names, emails, typed content, scenario text, answer text, or saved progress records are sent. Google also processes browser and connection metadata, including IP addresses in transit and approximate location, and uses session analytics cookies. No precise location is requested. Advertising features are disabled. Reports summarize participating visitors; these are not anonymous totals at collection.
